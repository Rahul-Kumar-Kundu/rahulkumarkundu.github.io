MetaSeg is a multimodal skin lesion segmentation framework that integrates patient demographic metadata into the decoder via FiLM modulation, improving segmentation performance across diverse patient populations.

Developed in collaboration with Qatar University. Targeting **Computers in Biology and Medicine** (Elsevier Q1).

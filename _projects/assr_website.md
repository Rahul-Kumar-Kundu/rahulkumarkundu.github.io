Led a team to design and build the official website for the **Astronomy and Science Society of RUET (ASSR)**, serving as the primary digital platform for the society's announcements, events, member management, and outreach activities.

The project involved full-stack web development from design to deployment, with a focus on performance, scalability, and ease of content management for non-technical society members.

**Key features:**
- Society announcements and news feed
- Event listing and management
- Member registration and profile management
- Admin dashboard for content control
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

**Tech stack:**
- **Frontend:** Next.js (React framework) with Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Next.js API routes with Prisma ORM
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Deployment:** Vercel

**Role:** Team Lead — responsible for architecture design, task delegation, frontend development, and deployment.

**Live Site:** [assr-ruet.vercel.app](https://assr-ruet.vercel.app/)

**Status:** Completed and live

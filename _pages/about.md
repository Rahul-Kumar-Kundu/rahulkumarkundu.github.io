<div style="text-align: justify;">

I am <strong>Rahul Kumar Kundu</strong>, a researcher in machine learning and trustworthy artificial intelligence. I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at <strong>Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET)</strong>, Bangladesh, graduating with a CGPA of 3.79/4.00 and ranking 7th among 60 students. I am currently working as a remote <strong>Research Assistant</strong> under <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SgMrFTIAAAAJ](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VC8FmyEAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Dr. Amith Khandakar</a> at <strong>Qatar University</strong>.

My research focuses on developing reliable, interpretable, and deployable machine learning systems. I am particularly interested in <strong>medical image analysis</strong>, <strong>multimodal learning</strong>, <strong>explainable AI</strong>, <strong>trustworthy AI</strong>, <strong>signal processing</strong>, and <strong>machine learning for cybersecurity</strong>. Across these areas, I aim to understand not only how models perform, but also how they generalize across datasets, respond to distribution shifts, and support dependable real-world decision-making.

My undergraduate thesis led to <strong>ED-Net</strong>, a deep learning framework for colorectal polyp segmentation incorporating multiscale feature extraction, attention mechanisms, interpretability analysis, and external validation across multiple clinical datasets. This work was published in <em>Biomedical Signal Processing and Control</em> (Elsevier Q1, IF: 4.9, 2026). I have also worked on demographic fairness in skin lesion segmentation, data leakage in clinical machine learning, explainable phishing detection, and lightweight image classification for agricultural applications.

In my current research role, I work on multimodal cattle behavior recognition using wearable and farm-sensing modalities under Dr. Khandakar's supervision. My work involves model development, cross-animal evaluation, interpretability, reproducible experimentation, and practical deployment under limited sensor availability. This experience has further strengthened my interest in multimodal and sensor-based AI systems that remain reliable under real-world constraints.

My long-term research goal is to develop trustworthy AI methods for healthcare and intelligent sensing, particularly for settings where data, computational resources, and specialist expertise are limited. I plan to pursue advanced research that combines methodological rigor with practical impact, and ultimately contribute to building stronger AI research capacity in Bangladesh.
